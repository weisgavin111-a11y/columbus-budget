    Where the money came from

    Columbus's income tax rate is 2.5%. By city code, 75% of collections go to the general fund and 25% to a separate fund for capital projects like roads and buildings. Most property tax must go to debt payments and police and fire pensions before any reaches the general fund.

      Terms used here

      General fund
      The city's main operating account for day-to-day services.
      Budgetary basis
      Counts money when it is spent or committed, which is how the city manages its budget. The audited annual report (ACFR) uses a different method, so its totals differ.
      Encumbrance
      Money set aside for something ordered but not yet paid for. If the order is cancelled, the money returns to the fund.
      Projected
      The city's estimate of where the current year will end, made before the year is over.
      Rainy-day fund
      The economic stabilization fund: savings kept for downturns and emergencies.
      Tickmarks
      An auditor's pencil marks showing how a figure was checked. F footed: the column adds up to the printed total. ✓ recomputed or agreed to another figure. ? open: a difference not yet explained.
